⚖️ Separation of Duties Guardrails & Governance Policy

Configure automated backend restrictions to enforce dual sign-off (Four-Eyes Principle) and prevent single reviewers from performing conflicting approval actions.

1. Four-Eyes Principle (Dual Sign-Off)
The staff member who performs the initial KYC/KYB document review CANNOT execute the final approval decision. Requires second reviewer.
2. Prevent Self-Approval on Escalations
A reviewer cannot approve a case they previously escalated. Escalated cases must be resolved by another authorized staff member.
3. Prevent Self-Moderation Decision Overrides
A review moderator cannot modify or override their own previous moderation decision without formal escalation.
4. High-Risk Cases Require Super Admin Approval
Cases flagged with High Risk (e.g. multiple TIN registrations or defamation claims) strictly require signoff from a Super Admin.
